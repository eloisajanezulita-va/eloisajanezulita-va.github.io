About me

Support that feels human

I'm a Social Media & Customer Support Virtual Assistant based in Governor Generoso, Davao Oriental, Philippines. I have hands-on experience managing social media accounts, creating branded graphics and short-form videos, and resolving high-volume customer inquiries across email, live chat and DMs.

Working as an Online ESL Instructor and Operations Manager & Support Coordinator taught me to communicate patiently, guide people step by step and keep everything organized with clear SOPs and trackers. I use AI tools like Claude, Gemini and ChatGPT every day to draft captions, support macros and documentation, so work stays fast and consistent.

I'm also a 4th-year BSED English major, certified at C2 Proficient English (EF SET 71/100), and ready to cover shifts across US, UK or APAC time zones.
